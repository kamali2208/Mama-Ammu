A Letter To
My Mama

My dearest Mama,

Sometimes I think about how a normal day can quietly become the beginning of something that changes our whole life. I never knew that one small meeting, one conversation and one simple “Hi” would slowly become such a beautiful part of my heart.

Unkooda pesura every small conversation-um enakku romba precious. Sometimes we talk about something serious, sometimes we laugh for no reason, sometimes we fight, and sometimes oru word kooda pesaama irundhaalum, your presence itself gives me comfort.

Naan perfect person illa mama. Enakku neraya mistakes irukkum, mood changes irukkum, sometimes unnecessary-aa kovam varum. Aana one thing mattum true — I genuinely care about you and I genuinely want you in my future.

Un smile paatha enakku automatically happiness varum. Un voice ketta konjam peaceful-aa feel pannuven. Un message varumbodhu even a normal day becomes a little more special.

Namma relationship perfect-aa irukkanum nu naan expect pannala. Instead, whatever comes, good days or difficult days, we should learn, understand and choose each other again and again. Adhu dhaan enakku real love.

One day when we look back at all these small memories, I want us to smile and say, “Idhellam namma life oda beautiful beginning.”

Mama, thank you for becoming someone I can trust, someone I can talk to, someone I can miss and someone I can love with my whole heart.

Enakku future pathi neraya dreams irukku. And those dreams-la nee irukkanum. Life enga kondu ponaalum, my heart always wants to find its way back to you.

I don't need a perfect love story. I just want our real story — with laughter, silly fights, hugs, memories, understanding and a lifetime of choosing each other.

With all my heart,
Kamali 💚

Happy Birthday My Mamaaa 💚

Innaiku un birthday mama... but enakku idhu just oru birthday illa. Indha world-ku nee vandha special day. And somehow, life-la oru point-la, nee en life-ku vandha person aayita. Adhu dhaan enakku biggest blessing.

Namma story epdi start aachunu yosicha, oru small meeting, oru simple conversation, oru “Hi”, and then slowly so many memories. I never expected that those little moments would become something this important to me.

Enakku un kooda irukkura small moments romba precious. Un call, un message, un smile, un voice, even namma silly fights kooda later memories aagidum.

Mama, un life-la nee aasapadura ellame unakku kedaikkanum. Un dreams successful aaganum. Unakku happiness, peace, good health, success and lots of beautiful moments always irukkanum.

Namma future-la innum neraya birthdays, neraya trips, neraya laughs, neraya photos, neraya hugs, and countless memories create pannanum.

Naan perfect-aa promise panna mudiyadhu. But one thing I can say honestly — I will always care about you, always value what we have, and always keep our memories close to my heart.

Nee enakku lover mattum illa mama. Nee en comfort, en safe place, en favourite person, en best friend.

Life full-ah un kooda travel pannanum. Good days-laum, difficult days-laum, happy moments-laum, silent moments-laum — I want us to keep choosing each other.

Happy Birthday once again, Thraveen. 🎂💚

May this new year of your life bring you everything your heart deserves.

Forever yours,
Kamali 💚♾️
